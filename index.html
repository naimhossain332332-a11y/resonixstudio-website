Frequently Asked Questions

What is Resonix Studio?

Resonix Studio is a desktop application for Windows that lets you generate unique sound effects using AI and manage large sound libraries with intelligent AI-powered search. Everything runs locally on your PC.

How does the AI sound generation work?

Resonix Studio uses MOSS-SoundEffect 2.0, an open-source AI model that generates sound effects from text descriptions. You type what you want (e.g., "thunder rumble with rain"), and the AI creates a unique sound effect.

Does it require internet?

Only for the initial download of AI models (~7 GB, one-time). After that, everything works 100% offline — sound generation, search, and library management.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10/11 (64-bit), 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended), 10 GB free disk space. Optional: Vulkan-compatible GPU for faster generation.

How does licensing work?

You receive a unique license key via WhatsApp after purchase. Enter it in the app to unlock. The key never expires, works offline, and can be transferred to another PC using Settings → Deactivate.
